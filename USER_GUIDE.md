# Student Guide to UniBike

Welcome! This system helps us keep our shared flatshare (and soon, university) bikes in top condition.

## How to Report a Broken Bike
1. Scan the QR code sticker on the bicycle using your smartphone camera.
2. The link will take you to the **QR Ticketing** tab of our app.
3. Enter the **Bike ID** (if not auto-filled) and select the issue you're experiencing from the dropdown (e.g., "Flat Tire").
4. Add any optional details in the description box.
5. Click **Submit Ticket**. The bike will be marked as "Out of Order" so others know it needs fixing.

## How to Fix a Bike Yourself!
Want to learn how to fix the bike? We have an AI Assistant!
1. Go to the **DIY Assistant** tab in the app.
2. Ask a question like, "How do I fix a flat tire?" or "How do I adjust the brakes?"
3. The AI will read through our official repair manuals and give you step-by-step instructions.
